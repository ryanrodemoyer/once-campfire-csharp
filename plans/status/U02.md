## Notes for dependents

- `Room.MessagesCount`: `Room` record includes `long MessagesCount = 0`, reading from `"rooms"."messages_count"`.
- `RoomMessagesCount`: `Campfire.Data.Schema.RoomMessagesCount` provides `Install`, `Uninstall`, `Backfill`, `Ensure`, and `TriggersInstalled` matching Rails `Room::MessagesCount`.
- Triggers installed on SQLite:
  - `messages_ai_rooms_messages_count`: AFTER INSERT ON messages
  - `messages_ad_rooms_messages_count`: AFTER DELETE ON messages
  - `messages_au_rooms_messages_count`: AFTER UPDATE OF room_id ON messages WHEN OLD.room_id IS NOT NEW.room_id
- `SchemaPreparer.Prepare`: invokes `EnsureAdditions` (creating `index_messages_on_room_id_and_created_at` and `index_messages_on_room_id_and_updated_at` if missing) and `RoomMessagesCount.Ensure` on both fresh databases and existing migrated databases.
- `ByBotsController.IndexAsync`: populates `X-Total-Count` directly from `CurrentRoom.MessagesCount` without querying `COUNT(*)`.
- `ByBotsReplayTests`: skipped until U04 because at edbc779 unread room notifications were moved to `Message::BroadcastUnreadRoomJob` (U04), and `by_bots.json` requires regeneration once U04 implements that job.
