## Notes for dependents

- `Campfire.Data.Pagination.MessagePages` (static, `SqliteSession` first, `roomId` second — every
  caller pages `room.messages`): `LastPage`, `FirstPage`, `PageBefore(message)`,
  `PageAfter(message)`, `PageAround(message)` (≤ 81), `PageCreatedSince(time)`,
  `PageUpdatedSince(time, excluding)` (pass the ids `PageCreatedSince` returned, as
  `@room.messages.without(@new_messages)`), `ExistsBefore`/`ExistsAfter` (Messages::ByBots'
  next-page params), `IsPaged` (`paged?`, as `SELECT 1 … LIMIT 1 OFFSET 40` rather than a full
  count). `PageSize` = 40. Pages are oldest first.
- The SQL is Active Record's: `last(n)` is `ORDER BY created_at DESC LIMIT 40` flipped in memory,
  so `created_at` ties break as they do in Rails; the bound time is `quoted_date` text
  (`Db.Time`), not the stored text, which matters for times SQLite's `STRFTIME` wrote.
- For a refresh, `since` is `Time.at(0, since.to_i, :millisecond)`:
  `DateTimeOffset.FromUnixTimeMilliseconds`.
- `with_presentation` preloading and `Page#preload_associations` are the renderer's concern (M03/M04).
  `searches_controller`'s `last_page_of(100)` over search results belongs with search (D04).
