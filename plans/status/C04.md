## What was built

- **ActiveRecord Signed IDs** (`src/Campfire.RailsCompat/SignedIds/SignedId.cs`):
  - `SignedId.Generate` and `SignedId.Verify` with salt `active_record/signed_id`.
  - Verifier with `SHA256` and URL-safe Base64, falling back to legacy `SHA1` with strict Base64 and `:json_allow_marshal`.
  - Purpose combination with camelCase-to-snake_case underscoring (`Rooms::Open` -> `rooms/open`, `HTTPRequest` -> `http_request`).
- **Transferable & Avatar Helpers** (`src/Campfire.RailsCompat/SignedIds/TransferableUser.cs`):
  - `GenerateTransferId` and `VerifyTransferId` with `TRANSFER_LINK_EXPIRY_DURATION = 4.hours`.
  - `GenerateAvatarSignedId` and `VerifyAvatarSignedId` without expiry.
- **Turbo Stream Names** (`src/Campfire.RailsCompat/SignedIds/TurboStreamName.cs`):
  - Key derivation for `turbo/signed_stream_verifier_key` (SHA256, strict Base64, raw JSON, no metadata envelope).
  - `SignedStreamName` and `VerifiedStreamName` joining streamables with `:`.
- **GlobalID & SignedGlobalID** (`src/Campfire.RailsCompat/GlobalId/GlobalId.cs`, `SignedGlobalId.cs`):
  - `GlobalId`: URI parser and formatter (`gid://campfire/<Model>/<id>`), `ToParam` and `FromParam` (unpadded URL-safe Base64).
  - `SignedGlobalId`: Key derivation for `signed_global_ids` (SHA1, URL-safe Base64 with padding, `:json_allow_marshal`).
  - Legacy self-validated metadata verification support (`globalid < 1.0`).
- **ActionText Invalid-SGID Fallback** (`src/Campfire.RailsCompat/GlobalId/InvalidSgidFallback.cs`):
  - Extracts user GIDs from expired or invalid signatures (e.g., after secret rotation).
  - Handles modern `_rails.data` and legacy Rails 7 Marshal `_rails.message` payloads (extracting `gid://campfire/User/<id>` without unsafe deserialization).
  - Whitelists only the `User` model, strictly rejecting all other models.
