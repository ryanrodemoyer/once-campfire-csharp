## Notes for dependents

- `ServerComposition.Compose` in `src/Campfire.Server/Composition/ServerComposition.cs` wires the production server:
  - Mounts `CableServer<User>` at `/cable` with `SessionCookieAuthenticator` and `RevocationGuard`.
  - In-process `JobRunner` sized by `JOB_CONCURRENCY` / `WEB_CONCURRENCY` (defaulting to ceil(ProcessorCount * 0.666)).
  - Registers all handlers: `BotWebhooks`, `RemoveBannedContent`, `MessagePusher`, `MessageAttachmentJobs`.
  - Builds `DomainSeams` using the real `CableServer` as `IBroadcaster`, `JobRunner` as `IJobQueue`, and `RevocationGuard` revoker as `IConnectionRevoker`.
  - Configures `WebApp.ResetRemoteConnections` to disconnect the user's cable connections (`revoker.Disconnect(user.Id, reconnect: true)`).
  - Registers services in `WebApplication` DI and registers `CompositionShutdownService` hosted service for graceful disposal of background runners and clients on shutdown.
