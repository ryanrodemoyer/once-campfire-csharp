## Notes for dependents

- `CableServer.Builder(config, authenticator).Channel("RoomChannel", () => new RoomChannel(...)).Logger(logger).Build()`
  gives a `CableServer<TUser>`. Mount it with `app.UseWebSockets()` ahead of it and
  `CableServer<TUser>.HandleAsync(context)` at `/cable` (`CableProtocol.DefaultMountPath`).
  Without `UseWebSockets`, every request is a 404. Dispose it on shutdown (stops the heartbeat).
  Campfire's authenticator is `SessionCookieAuthenticator(database, keys)` (the signed
  `session_token` cookie → `session.user`, identifier `gid://campfire/User/<id>`).
- Channels (RT02 Turbo, RT03 app channels) subclass `Channel<TUser>`: override
  `SubscribedAsync`, `UnsubscribedAsync` and `PerformAsync(action, data)` (return false for a
  name that isn't an action: "Unable to process"). Inside: `Params`, `CurrentUser`,
  `StreamFrom`/`StreamFor`/`StreamOrRejectFor`, `StopStreamFrom`, `Reject()`,
  `SubscriptionRejected`, `Transmit(json)`, `BroadcastTo(broadcastables, json)`. Put Rails'
  `on_subscribe`/`on_unsubscribe` callbacks at the end of those methods. A channel instance is
  used by one connection, one command at a time. Streams start delivering after the command's
  own frames (the confirmation) are queued. An exception from a callback is logged and nothing
  is sent, as in Rails. `EmptyChannel<TUser>` is `HeartbeatChannel` and
  `ApplicationCable::Channel`.
- `CableServer<TUser>` implements `IBroadcaster` (`Broadcast(stream, encodedPayload)`); there is
  also `Broadcast(broadcasting, JsonNode)` (Active Support JSON) and
  `BroadcastTo(className, broadcastables, json)`. `ChannelNaming.BroadcastingFor` gives
  `broadcasting_for` names. Broadcasts are delivered synchronously into each connection's
  bounded outbox; they never block.
- Remote disconnects: `Disconnect(connectionIdentifier, reconnect)`, e.g.
  `SessionCookieAuthenticator.ConnectionIdentifier(user.Id)`. RT04's `IConnectionRevoker` and
  sign-out (`WebApp.ResetRemoteConnections`) call it. `Restart()` closes everything with
  `server_restart`.
- `CableConfig`: `AssumeSsl` (on unless `DISABLE_SSL`), `DisableRequestForgeryProtection`
  (the performance environment sets it), `AllowedRequestOrigins`, `QueueCapacity` (256 frames
  per connection before a lag disconnect), `EnableCompression` (permessage-deflate, no context
  takeover), close timeout and message/subscription limits.
