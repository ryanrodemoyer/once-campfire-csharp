## What was built

- `Controllers/Rooms/SettingsController.cs`: `RoomSettingsController`, RoomsController's part
  the room namespace inherits: its callbacks (a subclass re-declaring one replaces it, as in
  Rails), `set_room` in a virtual `room_scope`, `ensure_can_administer`,
  `ensure_permission_to_create_rooms`, `destroy` (memberships deleted, each message destroyed
  through `MessageLifecycle.Destroy`, the room, then `broadcast_remove_to :rooms`), page
  rendering and the sidebar broadcasts. `RoomsDestroyController` is rooms#destroy.
- `OpensController.cs`, `ClosedsController.cs`, `DirectsController.cs`,
  `InvolvementsController.cs` and their `*Routes.cs`, which bind every route of the four
  controllers (opens/closeds/directs#index to RoomsController's index they inherit,
  directs#update to ActionNotFound). `SettingsRoutes.cs` binds rooms#destroy.
- Views: `rooms/layouts/_new`, `_edit`, `_form`; `rooms/opens/{new,edit,_form,_user}`;
  `rooms/closeds/{new,edit,_form,_user}`; `rooms/directs/{new,edit}`; `rooms/involvements/show`.

## Notes for dependents

- **M01 (sidebar):** the room settings broadcast `users/sidebars/rooms/_shared` and `_direct`,
  which are M01's. Until M01 lands they are stand-ins in `Views/rooms/layouts/_sidebar_shared`
  and `_sidebar_direct` (`View.RoomsSidebarShared`, `View.RoomsSidebarDirect`, matched byte for
  byte by the replay). When M01 ports them, the controllers can render M01's templates and the
  stand-ins go.
- **users/autocompletables/_template** (owned with the autocompletable users task) is inlined
  as `Views/rooms/directs/_autocompletable_template` for the same reason.
- Routing quirk kept from Rails: `GET /rooms/opens` (and closeds, directs) reaches rooms#show
  with id "opens" first, so it redirects to the root with "Room not found or inaccessible".
- The Q01 `rooms` replay family also reads `/users/me/sidebar`, so it can gate these routes once
  M01 lands.

## Known gaps
