## What was built

- `src/Campfire.Web/Controllers/Messages/BoostsController.cs`: `MessagesBoostsController` with
  boosts_controller.rb's `set_message` (`Current.user.reachable_messages.find`) and `set_boost`
  (`find_by!(id:, booster: Current.user)`); index and new render in the application layout, or
  turbo-rails' frame layout for a Turbo frame request; create (`boosts.create!` through
  Campfire.Data's `BoostLifecycle`, which touches the message and room and re-indexes the
  message, then `broadcast_append_to` the room with `maintain_scroll`, then the redirect); destroy
  (`destroy!`, `broadcast_remove_to`, then `head :no_content` as Rails' implicit render does
  without a template).
- `BoostsRoutes.cs` binds index, new, create and destroy, and answers show, edit and update with
  `AbstractController::ActionNotFound` (404).
- `BoostsViews.cs` and `src/Campfire.Web/Views/messages/boosts/{index,new}.html.erb.cs`.

## Known gaps

- **Fragment caching** (M02's gap): Rails serves a boost's cached `cache boost` fragment, so a
  boost first rendered by its broadcast shows a token-less delete form in later pages. We always
  render, with a token on pages. The replay leaves that token out on both sides.
