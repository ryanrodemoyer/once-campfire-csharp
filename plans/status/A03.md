## Notes for dependents

- **Ownership moves** (in `plans/tasks.yaml`): A03 took `users/profiles/_transfer*` from A02 and
  `accounts/users/_user*` and `_next_page_container*` from A04, since `users/show` and
  `accounts/edit` render them. A02 keeps `users/profiles/show*` and `_membership*`; A04 keeps
  `accounts/users/index*` and renders `View.AccountsUsersUser(w, user, avatarToken)` and
  `View.AccountsUsersNextPageContainer(w, page)`. Commented on #50 and #60.
- **`Controller.IsLive`** (W02's pipeline, see Outside `owns`): override it to `true` in a
  controller that includes `ActionController::Live` (directly or through
  `ActiveStorage::Streaming`). A06's avatars controller needs it too.
- **`form_with model: @account`** posts to `/account.<id>`: `polymorphic_path` of a record for the
  singular `resource :account` puts the id in the format. `accounts/edit` reproduces it
  (`AccountEditPage.AccountFormPath`).
- **Logo changes don't touch the account** in the reference: its `updated_at`, and so the logo's
  ETag and `fresh_account_logo_path`'s `?v=`, stay as they were. A cached logo can therefore be
  served for up to five minutes after a change, as in Rails.
- `View.AccountFormModel(account)` is the account's form model (`name`, `custom_styles`).
- `accounts/_help_contact` is still rendered from `Views/sessions/_help_contact.html.erb.cs`
  (`View.AccountsHelpContact`, declared in A01's `SessionsViews.cs`); A03 owns the path but moving
  the file would mean editing A01's declaration for no change in output.

## Outside `owns`

- `src/Campfire.Web/Pipeline/` (W02, done): `Controller.IsLive`, `ActionController::Live`'s
  response. `Live::Response.new` has none of the default headers (`X-Frame-Options` and the
  rest); a rendered body (`head`, `redirect_to`, `render`, not `send_file`) goes through
  `Live::Buffer#write`, which defaults `Cache-Control` to `no-cache` (so a 304 says `no-cache`);
  and `Live::Response#before_committed` writes the cookie jar once before the cookie middleware
  writes it again, so cookies set by the action are sent twice. Each is pinned by the replay.
- `plans/tasks.yaml`: the ownership moves above (A02, A04).
- Tests live in `tests/Campfire.Web.Tests/Controllers/Accounts/`.

## Known gaps

- Uploading an avatar or logo doesn't enqueue `ActiveStorage::AnalyzeJob`, and replacing or
  deleting a logo doesn't enqueue `ActiveStorage::PurgeJob` for the old blob (its row and file
  stay, as they do in the reference until the job runs): `Campfire.Data.Events` has no Active
  Storage jobs yet (A01's gap too). The rows the requests themselves write match.
- The logo variant bytes are compared against the reference run with this session's libvips
  (8.15.1); the canonical comparison with the pinned image's libvips 8.16.1 is S03's open
  criterion (P02).
