## What was built (`src/Campfire.RichText/Attachments/`)

- `AttachmentResolution`: Campfire's `ActionText::Attachment.from_node` — opengraph embed, else
  the unverified-SGID User fallback (`reference/lib/rails_ext/action_text_attachables.rb`, incl.
  Marshal-era payloads and Ruby's `split("--").first`), else `ActionText::Attachable.from_node`
  (signed SGID, content attachment, remote image) with Lexxy's RemoteVideo fallback.
- `AttachmentPartials`: `users/_mention` with `avatar_tag`, `_opengraph_embed`, and Action Text's
  and Lexxy's content attachment, remote image, remote video and missing-attachable partials.
- `RichTextRenderer`: `render_action_text_content` — content attributes sanitized, nodes rebuilt
  `with_full_attributes`, `inner_html=` and `Node#replace` round trips in Nokogiri's parse
  contexts, galleries, Action Text's sanitizer — and `Content#to_s` with the `lexxy-content` layout.
- `RailsAutoLink`: rails_autolink 1.1.8's `auto_link` exactly as `message_presentation` calls it
  (Ruby line anchors, ASCII `\w`, `SafeBuffer#+` escaping trailing punctuation, `mail_to`).
- `MessagePresentation`: the text branch of `message_presentation` and its rescue.

## Notes for dependents

- Callers implement `IAttachableResolver` over the database: `LocateSigned` is
  `GlobalID::Locator.locate_signed(sgid, for: "attachable")` (verify with C04's `SignedGlobalId`;
  report `MissingRecord` when the signature verifies but the record is gone), `FindGid` is
  `GlobalID.find`. `MentionUser` carries what the mention partial prints: `User#title`,
  `user_path`, `fresh_user_avatar_path` and a freshly minted `attachable_sgid`.
- M02: `MessagePresentation.Present(body, new RenderContext(resolver, requestHost))` returns
  `Presentation.Html` (embed as is) or `Presentation.Unrenderable` (render
  `messages/_unrenderable`). The attachment and sound branches of `message_presentation` are M02's.
- R05: `AttachmentResolution.AttachmentFromNode`, `Attachable.AttachableContentType` and
  `RichTextRenderer.RenderAttachment` are what `editable_body` needs;
  `AttachmentResolution.ActionTextAttachableFromNode` is `Content#attachables` (mentionees).
- `Sanitize/AutoLink.cs` (R02) doesn't match the reference: it escapes `<`/`>` in attribute
  values, relinks URLs inside existing links, links no email addresses and doesn't escape trailing
  punctuation, so 113 of the 658 presentations differed with it. `RailsAutoLink` replaces it here;
  reported on #20 so the R02 copy can be removed.
