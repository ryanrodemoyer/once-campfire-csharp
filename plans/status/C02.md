## Notes for dependents

- **CookieJar** (`Campfire.RailsCompat.Cookies.CookieJar`):
  - Created via `new CookieJar(secretKeyBase, clock)` or `CookieJar.FromHeaders(headers, secretKeyBase, clock)`.
  - Plain cookies accessed via indexer `jar[name]` or `jar.Set(name, value, options)`.
  - Signed cookies accessed via `jar.Signed[name]` and `jar.Signed.Set(name, value, options)`.
  - Encrypted cookies accessed via `jar.Encrypted[name]` and `jar.Encrypted.Set(name, node, options)`.
  - Permanent cookies via `jar.Permanent.Set(...)`, `jar.Signed.Permanent.Set(...)`, `jar.Encrypted.Permanent.Set(...)`.
  - Authentication helpers: `jar.SetAuthenticationCookie(token)` and `jar.RemoveAuthenticationCookie()`.
  - `jar.ToSetCookieHeaders(ssl, host)` formats headers conforming to Rack / Rails standards.
- **Session** (`Campfire.RailsCompat.Session.Session`):
  - Encrypted cookie store (`_campfire_session`), lazy loading.
  - Accessors: `session.Id`, `session.CsrfToken`, `session.ReturnToAfterAuthenticating`, `session.Flash`, `session[key]`.
  - `session.Commit()` only commits to cookie jar if modified; deletes cookie if only `session_id` remains.
  - `session.Reset()` matches Rails `reset_session`.
- **Flash** (`Campfire.RailsCompat.Session.Flash`):
  - Properties: `flash.Notice`, `flash.Alert`, `flash[key]`.
  - `flash.Now`: immediate request only.
  - `flash.Keep(key)` and `flash.Discard(key)`.
