## What was built

- `src/Campfire.Web/Controllers/Users/SidebarsController.cs`: `UsersSidebarsController` implementing `ShowAsync`, data loading for direct rooms, other (shared) rooms, and placeholder users (calculating `Math.Max(20 - excludeUserIds.Count, 0)`).
- `src/Campfire.Web/Controllers/Users/SidebarsRoutes.cs`: route mapping for `users/sidebars#show` via `Routes.UsersSidebarsShow`.
- `src/Campfire.Web/Views/users/sidebars/SidebarsViews.cs`: view models and view extensions for sidebar templates and helpers.
- `src/Campfire.Web/Views/users/sidebars/show.html.erb.cs`: main sidebar view template with Turbo cable stream sources, direct rooms section, shared rooms section, and sidebar tools.
- `src/Campfire.Web/Views/users/sidebars/rooms/_direct.html.erb.cs`: partial for direct rooms with avatar groups, avatars, and unread indicator.
- `src/Campfire.Web/Views/users/sidebars/rooms/_direct_placeholder.html.erb.cs`: partial for placeholder users to initiate a direct message (button_to form).
- `src/Campfire.Web/Views/users/sidebars/rooms/_shared.html.erb.cs`: partial for shared (open and closed) rooms with unread state.
- `tests/Campfire.Web.Tests/Controllers/UsersSidebars/`:
  - `Vectors/generate.rb` and `Vectors/sidebars.json`
  - `SidebarsReplayTests.cs`
  - `SidebarsReferenceTests.cs`
