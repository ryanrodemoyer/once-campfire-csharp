## Notes for dependents

- The seven engine routes in `Routes.g.cs` are bound by `Routes` in
  `src/Campfire.Web/Controllers/ActiveStorage/ActiveStorageRoutes.cs`. Q02 should see no 501
  on `/rails/active_storage/*`.
- Controllers inherit `Controller`, not `ApplicationController`. Disk `show` is public and sets
  `Cache-Control: max-age=3600, public` on every show response, including 304, 404, 416 and
  HEAD. Disk `update` and direct uploads require a session. Direct uploads also require CSRF;
  disk skips forgery protection in both directions.
- Blob and representation redirects expire in five minutes and send the disk URL. Blob proxy
  streams with `Accept-Ranges: bytes` and the forever cache (`max-age=3155695200, public,
  immutable`, etag of the path). A `Range` on the blob proxy is 206 or 416 and `no-cache`.
  Representation proxy has the forever cache and no `Accept-Ranges`; a `Range` is ignored.
  Those streamed responses are live, so they carry no default security headers and no
  `Content-Length`.
- Direct-upload JSON follows `schema.rb` column order, then `attachable_sgid`, `signed_id` and
  `direct_upload`. A missing metadata hash is SQL NULL and `{}` in the JSON.

## Known gaps

- The proxy multipart boundary is `SecureRandom.hex` on the reference, so that one body is
  compared by size and boundary length, not by sha256. The disk multipart boundary is the
  fixed Rack boundary, and that body matches by sha256.
- Responses where the reference omits `Content-Length` are not failed when this pipeline adds
  one. Lengths the reference sends are compared.
- Purge and the analysis jobs are not these endpoints.
